⚡ Quick Setup (one-time)

  1. Install Ollama — runs open-weight AI locally on your machine.
  2. Pull a vision model: ollama pull llama3.2-vision
  3. Enable CORS for browser access:
    OLLAMA_ORIGINS=* ollama serve
  4. Come back here, take a photo on the trail, and get instant identification — no internet needed!
